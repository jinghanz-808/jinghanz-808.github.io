About

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at the University of Utah. I currently work as an undergraduate research assistant in the IoTrust Lab, where I work with egocentric video, IMU, and neural data.

Before that, I spent a year as a full-stack intern at iLumens in Salt Lake City, where I worked on an IoT platform. I enjoy learning new tools when a project needs them, and I like building things that are complete and polished enough to share.

Research

At the moment, my research is about real-world navigation and how people move through everyday spaces.

Events in video

I work with first-person video and look for meaningful events in it, using both annotated events and unsupervised clustering of video features.

Brain and behavior

I work with intracranial EEG recordings collected during navigation, and I compare them with video, movement, and gaze.

Data collection

I help turn manual checks into repeatable steps, so we can plan the next round of data collection more clearly.
